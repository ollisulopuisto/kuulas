The transcript is the words of every speech track, in time order, with the speaker in the margin. It follows the play cursor, and the words being heard are underlined.

- Select words with the mouse or with Shift and the arrow keys; the same stretch is selected on the timeline.
- Delete the selected words: {key:edit.delete}. They are struck through and the gap stays.
- Cut them: {key:edit.cut}. Before the cut, the transcript shows what else would go: words other speakers say in the same stretch.
- {key:transport.play} still plays and stops; {key:panel.home} returns to the timeline.

Words removed by a cut leave a small marker; the Cuts list can put them back. See also [[clip]].
