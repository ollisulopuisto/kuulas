Every track is a lane, and time runs left to right. A click anywhere in the lanes moves the play cursor there. A click on a clip also selects it; Shift+click adds or removes a clip.

## Listen
- Play or stop: {key:transport.play}. Space always belongs to the timeline, whichever panel you are in.

## Zoom and move
- Close or wide view: {key:view.toggle} {show:view-toggle}
- Zoom in or out: {key:view.zoom_in} or {key:view.zoom_out}
- Fit the selection, or the whole edit: {key:view.fit}
- Back to the view before: {key:view.back}

Density, waveform and follow settings are in the View menu. {show:view-menu}

## Panels
{key:panel.next} moves the keyboard to the next panel; {key:panel.home} brings it back here.

See also [[clip]] and [[track]].
