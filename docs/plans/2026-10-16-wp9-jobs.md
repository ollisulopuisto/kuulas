# WP9 Background Jobs and Local Transcription

`Session.jobs` runs bounded background tasks and keeps their progress, elapsed
time estimate, outcome, and translated error. Jobs can be cancelled
cooperatively. While the live transport reports playback or there has been
recent user input, worker chunks yield so only one task proceeds at a time.

`POST /api/transcribe` creates one local job per media item. A job reads a
verified linked source or the project's working FLAC, uses faster-whisper
with a CPU thread allocation per worker, and commits one transcript revision
when complete. The job checks the transcript revision captured at submission
and reports a conflict rather than overwriting newer words. `GET /api/jobs`
and `DELETE /api/jobs/{id}` expose status and cancellation; the live channel
publishes job updates. Colab remains deferred.
