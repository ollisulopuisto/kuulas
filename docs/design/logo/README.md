# kuulas mark

Status: direction chosen by the owner (2026-10-18), not final. Sketches were
published as a private Artifact page during the design session.

The mark: a disc made of vertical bars (a waveform) with the *Vihreä kuulas*
apple cut out of it. The centre bar is missing (a cut), and the leaf is
cut into the next bar so it touches the bars cleanly (base merged into the
apple's shoulder, tip inside a bar, no slivers).

- `kuulas-mark-9.svg`: nine bars, from 48 px up.
- `kuulas-mark-7.svg`: seven bars, for 32 px and the dock.
- `kuulas-mark-5.svg`: five bars, the small-size version.

Open: nothing yet reads at 16 px (the leaf is lost below about 32 px); the
favicon needs its own simplified drawing. Colours are placeholders (apple
`#8CBF3F`), not yet tied to `ui/tokens.css`. App icon exports (macOS,
Windows) are not made yet.
